## PhD application description

I developed a five-fold benchmark comparing AtomSegNet, U-Net++, HRNet, and SwinUNet on 14,364 simulated HAADF-TEM images. Direct SwinUNet gave the strongest image reconstruction, while direct AtomSegNet produced the highest segmentation IoU. I then checked atomic-coordinate extraction, controlled sub-pixel shifts, and exploratory strain diagnostics on smaller selected samples, documenting where attractive reconstructions do not establish measurement accuracy.
